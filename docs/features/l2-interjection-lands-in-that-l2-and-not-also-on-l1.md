## Contract

One interjection addressed to a live L2 is delivered to that L2 once, in time to affect that live turn. It must not also enqueue a second copy on the L1 prompt queue.

A missing session still says the session is gone and does not queue the text on the L1.

Mid-turn Enter that is not addressed to an L2 is still an L1 interject. Do not break that.

Delivery that arrives too late to affect the live turn does not meet the contract. A second copy on the L1 prompt queue does not meet the contract either.
